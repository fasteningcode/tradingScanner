Understanding Volume Dry-Up
What It Means

Volume dry-up signals institutional accumulation. Smart money is quietly buying, absorbing supply, causing volume to drop while price consolidates in a tight range.

Best Setups

Look for stocks in Stage 2 (advancing trend) with Extreme/Strong classification. These have the highest probability of explosive breakouts.

Action Plan

Monitor these stocks for volume expansion. When volume increases by 2x+ on an upward move, it confirms institutional buying. Use proper stop losses.
