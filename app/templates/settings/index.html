Stage Analysis (Weinstein Methodology)

What is Stage Analysis?

Stage Analysis is a systematic method developed by Stan Weinstein to identify the current trend stage of a market index or sector:

  • Stage 1 (Accumulation): Basing pattern, preparing for uptrend
  • Stage 2 (Markup): Strong uptrend, advancing
  • Stage 3 (Distribution): Topping pattern, preparing for downtrend
  • Stage 4 (Decline): Downtrend, declining

Volume Dry-Up Analysis

What is Volume Dry-Up Analysis?

Volume dry-up is an institutional-grade pattern that identifies accumulation phases before major breakouts:

  • Criterion 1: Volume < 50% of 20-day average (sellers exhausted)
  • Criterion 2: Price consolidation < 6% over 5 days (tight range)
  • Signal: Low volume + tight price = Smart money accumulation → Breakout ready
Volume Dry-Up Scanner

Scan all stocks with historical data to find volume dry-up patterns. Stocks that qualify show institutional accumulation and are prime candidates for breakouts.

Range: 20-80%. Lower = More strict
Range: 2-15%. Lower = Tighter coil
